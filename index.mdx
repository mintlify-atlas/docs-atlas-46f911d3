---
title: "krunvm"
description: "Create and manage lightweight microVMs from OCI images"
---

krunvm is a CLI utility that creates and manages lightweight microVMs from OCI container images. It uses [libkrun](https://github.com/containers/libkrun) as the hypervisor backend and [buildah](https://github.com/containers/buildah) to fetch and mount OCI images as the root filesystem — no disk image creation or management required.
